A sample of how we work end to end — starting from a commercial problem, not a product spec.

FMCG · Retail

Nationwide Shelf Display Rollout

Naija Foods Co. — Marketing Lead: Chiamaka Eze
Brief

Redesign and deploy in-store shelf displays across 40+ retail outlets in a single push, with zero deviation from brand guidelines store to store.

Approach

Centralised design and production run, pre-scheduled installation crews per region, and a single QA checklist applied at every site before sign-off.

Result
40+ stores live on schedule 100% brand-consistent Zero reprints
Automotive · Fleet

Full Fleet Wrap Programme

Zenith Motors — Operations Manager: Tolu Ogunleye
Brief

Wrap a working delivery fleet without pulling vehicles out of service for more than a day each, using a finish durable enough for daily Lagos traffic.

Approach

Pre-fabricated wrap panels cut from finalised artwork, staggered vehicle scheduling, and a lamination grade selected for UV and abrasion resistance.

Result
Concept to install < 2 weeks Finish intact after 12 months Zero fleet downtime overrun
Retail · OOH

Highway Billboard & Installation

Apex Retail Group — Brand Manager: Bashir Abdullahi
Brief

Own high-traffic expressway sightlines ahead of a seasonal retail push, from artwork through structural installation.

Approach

Site selection against traffic and dwell-time data, in-house artwork production, and a certified installation crew for the structural mount.

Result
Live before campaign launch Visibility jump reported by client

Design and staff a booth that functioned as both a brand showcase and a live lead-capture point across a 3-day expo.
