Privacy Policy

How Creative Branding Solutions collects, uses and protects information submitted through this website.

Last updated: August 2026 — placeholder draft, pending legal review.

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a quote request, contact form, mockup upload or WhatsApp enquiry through this site, we may collect your name, company, email address, phone number, project details and any files (such as logos or artwork) you choose to share with us.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to quote requests and enquiries
  • To prepare mockups, proposals and invoices
  • To coordinate production, delivery and installation
  • To send updates about your order or project

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

3. WhatsApp & Third-Party Tools

Enquiries sent via the WhatsApp button or enquiry cart on this site are transmitted through WhatsApp's platform, which has its own privacy practices. Uploaded mockup files are used solely to prepare your quote.

4. Data Retention

We retain enquiry and project information for as long as reasonably necessary to service your account and meet our legal and accounting obligations.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information we hold about you by contacting us at hello@creativebrandingsolutions.com.ng.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@creativebrandingsolutions.com.ng or +234 806 000 1122.
