Our Story

Built To Run Campaigns, Not Just Fill Orders

Creative Branding Solutions started with a simple frustration: too many great Nigerian businesses were being let down by vendors who could execute one piece of a campaign — a billboard, a mug, a wrap — but couldn't own the whole brief.

Today we're the strategic execution partner for POSM, outdoor advertising, vehicle and environmental branding, experiential activation and corporate gifting — planned as one commercial conversation, not four separate RFQs. Backed by the ECS Group's in-house manufacturing, and led by a team that has run campaigns across Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt.
